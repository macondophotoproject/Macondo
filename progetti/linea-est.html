Eastern Line

Stations and villages along the railway that climbs from Luxembourg towards Trier — a portrait of the province, one stop at a time.

01

Desolation

Small, empty stations, two tracks, closed shelters, the grey overcast light typical of the region.

02

Waiting

Active, of the traveller; passive, of the bench and the station itself, which are waiting too.

03

Province

Agricultural, half-empty villages at the edge — a geographic border and a state of mind at once.
